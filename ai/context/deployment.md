Every deploy's smoke step runs the Worker's own indexing pass (POST
`/api/index`): the sample images seeded into the environment's R2 bucket, and
every image there titled and described by Workers AI's Llama 4 Scout and
embedded by BGE base, through the Worker's AI binding. Workers AI bills the
Cloudflare account the Worker runs in, after its free daily allocation; an
image already indexed is never described again. No AI vendor is brokered by
the platform, and none is needed.
